## Voice, captions and rendering

English uses Microsoft Jenny and Mandarin uses Xiaoxiao, via a pinned offline editing client; the provider does not expose its model revision. Proper nouns and several phrases were regenerated. The retained files include provider word boundaries, original MP3/PCM, selected hashes and rejected earlier candidates. Only trailing silence was trimmed, with at least 180 ms after the last active audio window and 150 ms after the final provider word. There is no pitch or speech-speed transformation. Encoding applies two-pass loudness normalization with AAC headroom.

Four caption tracks contain every authored word, grouped by meaning rather than a character-count timing estimate. Native-language word boundaries determine timings; matching translated groups share those phrase intervals. Continuous speech now permits a caption boundary at the exact shared word edge without dropping a word. Provider metadata is checked against independent, unprompted Whisper-small decoding of all sixteen selected clips. Seven ASR review notes include traditional/simplified Chinese spelling and proper-name substitutions. These are retained, not relabeled as perfect recognition. The session cannot listen to audio, so this is technical/content verification, not a native-speaker listening certification.

The chart shows all daily points and pullbacks, reaching the final result before a held ending. The spoken ending fits its final card. On portrait phones the native player is 4:3 around the 16:9 image; native captions move above the paused controls. The desktop caption line also clears the price-condition card. This adjustment preserves caption-language preferences and does not preload or autoplay media.
