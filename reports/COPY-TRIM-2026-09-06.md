# Site copy and information density · 2026-09-06

Owner request: shorten the whole website in Chinese and English and remove operational reporting from the customer experience. Screenshot reference: Radar market context, with collection counts, repeated explanations and raw timestamps above the first useful topic.

## Changes

- Edited 250 existing bilingual keys across the homepage, footer, authentication, watchlist, charts, alerts, billing, research, calendar earnings, market context, briefing, creator research, notifications, social tracking, public record and trending pages. Added four disclosure/result labels. Reviewed with humanizer and humanizer-zh.
- Market topics lead with one interpretation, explicitly labeled as an inference. Source counts, watchlist matching notes, uncertainties and next checks are available inside closed details. Stale/failed-source notices remain visible. Missing counts remain unknown, while real zeros are preserved.
- Removed the duplicate Pro access banner. Screening counts and rules, briefing data coverage, social collection counts and creator processing charts are secondary details. Methods, dates, source links, losses and unavailable states remain accessible.
- Removed a repeated three-step homepage workflow and made FAQ answers collapsed by default. Shortened hero, feature explanations, subscription notes and footer. Privacy terms and calculation assumptions were retained.
- Market, prediction-market and earnings timestamps use readable UTC dates without milliseconds; date-only values do not acquire a fabricated time.
- Copy policy recorded in the backend repository’s COPY-GUIDE.md. No data producer, scoring rule, API entitlement, paid data gate or delivery behavior was changed.
